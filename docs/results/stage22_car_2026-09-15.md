# Stage 22: candidate brains on the car platform (2026-09-15)

Platform: ACEBOTT QD001 + QD003 kit car (Plan §20.5.1), modelled by the
`car` sensor preset: one ultrasonic on a servo swept over three headings,
no bumper (a front ray under 0.7 u counts as contact), line module as cliff
sensors, a binary IR wall sensor on the right, odometry integrated from the
commanded wheels, no gyro, the dock marker seen by the camera within
+-30 degrees and 5 u. Benchmark as in A2: apartment maps 3000-3005, clean
task, 800 steps, 3 sensor-noise repeats, reward v2, needs layer with dock
autopilot and bumper reflex. Rows: `docs/results/benchmark_car_2026-09-15/`.

What changed against the vacuum preset:

- Survival fell from 0.93-0.98 to 0.5-0.9 for everyone: dock trips on
  command odometry with a 60-degree marker beacon miss the dock more often.
  This is the platform, not the brains.

- The scripted driver, which was the near-ceiling on the vacuum (56.7),
  drops to 34.9: the layer's own escape and homing rely on the bumper and on
  odometry that no longer tell the truth.

## Reading the table

- **Two brains share the top and are tied within their spread**: PPO
  retrained on the car preset (43.0 ± 4.0) and the vacuum-trained curriculum
  worm used as is (41.2 ± 0.2). The worm is the more stable of the two
  (spread 0.2 vs 4.0) and hits things half as often (14 vs 31 collisions).
- **Retraining the worm on the car preset made it worse** (41.2 -> 30.1).
  Its training fitness rose to 68 on the three training maps with one
  noise repeat, so evolution fitted those maps rather than the platform. The
  vacuum-trained brain generalises better than a brain tuned on the car
  preset with the same budget.
- **PPO needs the retraining**: transferred it is near the floor (27.3, 63
  collisions), retrained it is at the top. It learned the five-ray, real-bumper
  input and does not degrade gracefully when the inputs change.
- The rnn ends at 30 either way. The real worm from scratch (34.3) beats the
  transferred PPO and rnn, again the "Real > Random-class nets" of Plan §50.
- Survival is 0.7-0.9 for every brain (0.93-0.98 on the vacuum): the dock
  trips on command odometry with a 60-degree marker beacon are the platform's
  weak point, not the brains'.

## Decision for the car

The car drives with the **vacuum-trained curriculum worm under the needs
layer** (`docs/results/brains_a2/worm_from_worm_evolved_random.json`):
reward tied with the best PPO, half the collisions, no torch on the laptop,
and collisions are what will physically stop a car that has no bumper.
**PPO retrained on the car preset** (`docs/results/brains_car/ppo.json`) is
the second brain to put on the machine, as the A2 winner's representative.
The first real logs (stage 22.2) decide between them; the decision is
revisited when the sim-vs-real comparison changes the preset.


## 3. Layer v2 for the camera marker (2026-09-16, stage 22.1e)

Diagnosis on the section-2 rows: the first dock trip nearly always docks
(odometry still fresh), the second and third fail: dead reckoning has drifted
by 2-8 units, the car reaches 2-4 units from the dock, the marker is outside
the camera's 60-degree view and it circles on a blind map until the battery
is flat. The simulator also let the camera see the marker through walls,
which was fixed first (line of sight required).

Three changes in the engineered layer, camera presets only (vacuum rows are
untouched and reproduce):

- any marker sighting is trusted for homing (it is line of sight), where the
  IR beacon needed strength >= 0.5;
- every sighting re-anchors the dock estimate from the marker's sector and
  apparent size (distance = 1 / strength);
- near the estimated dock with no sighting, the layer spins a full turn on the
  odometry heading, moves 8 ticks, and looks again.
