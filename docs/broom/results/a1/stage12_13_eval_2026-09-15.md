# Stages 12-13: generalisation to unseen random maps and to targets

Six unseen random maps (seeds 2000-2005, 5 obstacles, 400-step cap, hunger
ends an episode at 250 ticks unless food resets it). Sums over the six maps,
rewards and ticks are means. Brains: stage 10 = trained on the fixed map;
stage 12 = stage 10 continued for 25 generations on random maps 0-3.

| task | brain | food | targets | damage | collisions | mean reward | mean ticks | distance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| food | stage 10 (fixed map) | 7 | 0 | 0 | 16 | -3.1 | 320 | 235 |
| food | stage 12 (random maps) | 6 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0.1 | 318 | 177 |
| target | stage 10 | 0 | 3 | 0 | 0 | -12.2 | 250 | 181 |
| target | stage 12 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 59 | -20.6 | 250 | 132 |
| target + 1 danger (untrained on danger) | stage 10 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 | -15.2 | 250 | 189 |
| target + 1 danger (untrained on danger) | stage 12 | 0 | 1 | 14 | 0 | -20.7 | 167 | 108 |

generations; the curriculum run (from the stage-10 brain) went 21 -> 27 on
