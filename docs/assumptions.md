| 2026-09-14 | 5 | Gap junction pair (a, b) becomes two positive electrical synapses a->b and b->a. | 2026-09-14 | 5 | Graded neuron mode added (`Neuron.graded`): `activity = clip(potential / threshold, 0, 1)`, no reset. Binary mode stays default for stages 0-4 code; connectome networks are graded. | 2026-09-14 | 6 | Network stability: with graded neurons the per-hop steady-state gain is `gain/decay`. Measured: gain ≥ 1.0 (decay 0.5) drives all 302 neurons to an all-on state that never switches off; gain 0.5 barely reaches AVA. Defaults set to `gain=0.45, decay=0.5` (0.9 per hop): a stimulus spreads to ~250 neurons and fades to silence within ~70 ticks after it stops. | Almost all synapses are excitatory (only 26 GABA sources); recurrent excitation with gain ≥ 1 is a fixed point. Training will move weights, so this is an initialisation choice, not a law. |
| 2026-09-14 | 6 | Per-target normalisation dilutes strong inputs into hubs: ASHL→AVAL is 15 of AVAL's 894 incoming sections, so AVA responds weakly (peak ~0.04) while low-input targets of ASH (ASK, ADL, AIA) respond strongly (~0.2). Propagation is read as ranking and timing, not absolute level. | Known artefact; alternative normalisations tried (global scale with cap) were unstable. Revisit at stage 8 when motor readout needs AVA/AVB levels. |
