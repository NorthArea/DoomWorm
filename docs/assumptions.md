| 2026-09-16 | B2/B11 | **Why the shuffled control beats the real connectome at shooting: a bottleneck we imposed, not a fact about wiring.** Measured on untrained brains (`doom4`, 3 maps x 200 ticks, before any evolution): the real connectome pulls the trigger in 0.0 % of ticks (mean fire 0.151), the degree-preserving shuffle in 77.0 % (0.679), the random topology in 34.8 %.
